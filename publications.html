Authored Book

Published Book · Dataset Curation & Applied ML

Sculpting Data for ML: The first act of Machine Learning

Endorsed with forewords by: Prof. Julian McAuley · UC San Diego → Laurence Moroney · Google AI Lead → Dr. Mengting Wan · Microsoft →

In the contemporary world of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, data is the new oil. For Machine Learning algorithms to work their magic, it is imperative to lay a firm foundation with relevant data. Sculpting Data for ML introduces readers to the first act of Machine Learning: Dataset Curation. This book puts forward practical strategies to identify valuable signal from the extensive amount of crude data available at our fingertips. The step-by-step guide pairs Python code examples from the extraction of real-world datasets with deep dives into how data fits into the broader Machine Learning ecosystem and shapes production model performance.
